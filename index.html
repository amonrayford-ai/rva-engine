Richmond, Virginia

Your city, built like a toy box.

The RVA Engine puts Richmond in one pocket app: tonight’s sky over the skyline, a swipe deck of things to do, a 3D board of eight neighborhoods, local feeds and the everyday tools you reach for.

Open the app Install on your phone

Free, runs in your browser, works offline after the first visit. No account, no tracking: everything you save stays on your device.

What’s inside

City sky

The home screen is the Richmond skyline under a real sky: sunrise, sunset and the moon phase are computed for the city’s coordinates and Eastern time, with the clock and a short read on the night ahead.

Swipe deck

Tonight’s plans as cards. Swipe right or tap the heart for “I’ll be there”, swipe left to skip, or tap the question mark for maybe. Filter by music, food, arts or outdoors; your picks are saved on your device for later.

3D City Board

Eight neighborhoods as little dioramas on a game board, from The Fan and Scott’s Addition to Church Hill and Shockoe Bottom. Tap one to zoom in and see what’s on there. Phones without 3D graphics get a fast 2D board instead.

Feeds

Food, Canvas, Banter, Work, Flashback, Movies, Shows, Rec, Trails, Wheels and Feet: eleven local feeds, each one tap away on its own tile, with nothing hidden off the edge of the screen.

Life tools

Watch alerts, holidays, date-night ideas, accessibility notes, transit times and a day planner: the small tools that make a city easier to live in.

Install on your phone

Installed, the RVA Engine opens full screen from your home screen like any app, and keeps working without a connection.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then Install.

On a Windows PC

The desktop app is the same RVA Engine in its own window, fully offline.
